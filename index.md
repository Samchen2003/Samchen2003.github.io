## About Me

I am a second-year DPhil student in the Torr Vision Group at the University of Oxford, supervised by Dr. Adel Bibi and Prof. Philip Torr. My research is generously funded by Epic Games. Before Oxford, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, where I was advised by Prof. Baoyuan Wu. I also spent a wonderful year as a visiting student at Oxford in 2023–2024, working closely with Dr. Qian Xie. My research interests lie broadly in AI safety and building AI scientists.

## Research Interests

- **AI Safety:** human red-teaming, automated red-teaming, and building defenses
- **AI Scientist:** automated research and automated peer review
